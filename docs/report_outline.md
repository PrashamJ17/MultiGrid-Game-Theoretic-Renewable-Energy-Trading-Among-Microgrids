# Report outline

The filled-in report material is in [report.md](report.md). The figures it embeds are in
[figures/](figures/) and are regenerated by `scripts/make_figures.py` from the saved experiment results.

1. Problem and motivation: P2P renewable trading among grid-connected microgrids.
2. Related work: the blueprint section 21 sources, cited as background only; none of their findings are presented as simulator output.
3. Formulation: players, energy model, tariffs, baseline, shared settlement (`model_specification.md`).
4. Cooperative market: LP allocation and bilateral Nash bargaining, and why optimal allocation is not a Nash equilibrium.
5. Competitive market: the seller price game, the buyer rule, pure-NE verification, `NO_PURE_NE` and tie sensitivity.
6. Software architecture and correctness gates: 345 tests, the export audit, and the audit report (`audit_report.md`).
7. Experiments E1–E7 (`experiment_protocol.md`) and their results, with every synthetic assumption labelled.
8. Discussion: findings vs modelling assumptions.
9. Limitations: no grid physics, storage or carbon claims; synthetic data.
10. Conclusions and extensions.

Demo and viva preparation: [demo_script.md](demo_script.md).
